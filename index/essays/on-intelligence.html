“On intelligence”,

2026 May 18, 09:08

Knowledge is intrinsic to its possessor; intelligence is a relative concept. If you are the only living being in the entire universe, you are simultaneously the smartest in existence, stupidest in existence, and neither; the concept of intelligence requires comparison, and without comparison it cancels itself out; not unlike how some argue the universe can’t have an age because “age” is a comparative concept. ♦
