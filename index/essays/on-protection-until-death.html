“On protection until death”,

2026 May 18, 08:58

I now think, essentially, that you are protected naturally until the coalescence of factors that occur on the day of your death. Not by a god, just by nature. And I now realise this is false because people get into traumatic accidents, go into comas, and die later. The “coalescence” could be stretched to mean the family’s decision (or doctors’ decision) to cut off life support on that specific day, but not really. ♦
